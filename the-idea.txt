[aventigroup](https://aventigroup.com/blog/product-led-sales-redefining-growth-strategies-in-cybersecurity/)

## Honest evaluation

The core thesis is valid: open-source, developer-first security products can win through bottom-up adoption. Snyk grew through developer-first PLG and later used product-qualified leads; Tailscale’s growth shows how simplicity and free usage can create viral adoption; Filigran shows that open-source credibility can scale to thousands of organizations. [theglobeandmail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/article-tailscale-tech-vpn-avery-pennarun/)
But your current concept tries to cover **code, dependencies, cloud runtime, AI agents, auto-remediation, threat intel, compliance, and dashboarding** all at once. That creates a dangerous scope problem: the product may be impressive but not irresistible, and in cybersecurity “almost useful” usually loses to a smaller tool that solves one painful job exceptionally well. [ventureinsecurity](https://ventureinsecurity.net/p/plg-is-an-oasis-not-a-mirage-making)

## What should change

The best move is to **start with one sharp wedge** and let the platform grow from that. My recommendation is to begin with: **autonomous software supply-chain protection for GitHub/GitLab + CI/CD**, focused on detecting risky dependency changes, malicious package behavior, secret leakage, and safe automated remediation. That wedge is easier to explain, easier to install, and much more likely to create clear proof of value in minutes. [securityweek](https://www.securityweek.com/cyber-insights-2025-open-source-and-the-software-supply-chain/)

## Best product direction

I would reshape the idea into this:

**Aegis Core**: an open-core, developer-first security agent that monitors code changes, dependency risk, CI/CD behavior, and release integrity, then explains and safely auto-fixes low-risk issues.

Why this is better:
- It is narrow enough to build well.
- It fits PLG because developers can install it without procurement.
- It creates natural expansion into cloud runtime, AI agents, and compliance later.
- It gives you a credible “security with proof” story instead of a vague “AI protects everything” story. [openviewpartners](https://openviewpartners.com/blog/snyk-plg-strategy/)

## Branding choice

For the name, I would favor **Aegis Core** over `cyberagents.app`. `cyberagents.app` sounds generic and slightly overhyped, while `Aegis Core` feels more serious, security-native, and enterprise-friendly. If you want a domain-style brand, I would lean toward **aegiscore.dev** for the developer wedge, then later extend into enterprise with a product family. The .dev ending also better matches the developer-first entry point implied by successful PLG security products. [openviewpartners](https://openviewpartners.com/blog/snyk-plg-strategy/)

## Zero-marketing reality

Starting with zero marketing is possible, but **not zero distribution work**. You still need a growth system built into the product: GitHub App install, one-command setup, great docs, demo repo, Slack alerts, public roadmap, and a free tier that delivers value before asking for payment. The strongest cybersecurity PLG companies do not rely on ads, but they absolutely rely on product design, integrations, community, and trust signals. [filigran](https://filigran.io/why-opencti-is-the-future-proof-tool-cisos-need-for-2025/)

## What matters most

The single most important thing is **time-to-trust**. In cybersecurity, users do not just ask “Does it work?” They ask “Can I trust it with my repos, cloud, and alerts?” Your product must prove value in minutes, be transparent enough to inspect, and avoid false positives that make people uninstall it. If trust is weak, no amount of “zero marketing” will save it. [soroni.co](https://soroni.co.uk/why-cybersecurity-sales-cycles-are-so-long-and-how-to-shorten-them/)

## My recommendation

If you want the highest probability path to becoming category-leading, build this sequence:
1. Start with **supply-chain and CI/CD security** for developers.
2. Make the OSS version instantly useful and safe.
3. Add **one-click remediation** for low-risk issues.
4. Use enterprise pricing for audit, policy, multi-team, and advanced AI features.
5. Expand later into runtime, cloud posture, and AI-agent defense only after the wedge is loved.
